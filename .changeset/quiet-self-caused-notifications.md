Afterhours no longer chimes and posts a banner when you flip its switch, pause it or resume it from the menu, and each kind of notice replaces the last one in Notification Center instead of piling up. ⌥⌘L still tells you what it did. When macOS has notifications turned off for Afterhours, Settings says so and offers to open the system setting.
