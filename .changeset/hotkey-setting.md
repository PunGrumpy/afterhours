You can turn off the ⌥⌘L shortcut in Settings > General, so it stops taking those keys from other apps, like Reformat Code in JetBrains IDEs or Downloads in Finder and Safari. Pressing ⌥⌘L now always tells you whether it turned Afterhours on or off, and Settings tells you when another app already uses the shortcut.
