The menu bar mug now looks different when Afterhours is off than when it is idle, so a glance tells you whether it will keep your Mac awake. The Pause buttons are unavailable while Afterhours is off, and turning it back on no longer lands in a stale pause. VoiceOver now hears the icon's state, which 0.3.4 promised but didn't deliver.
